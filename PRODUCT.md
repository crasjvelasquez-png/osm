# Open Sound Meter

## Register

product

## Users

Audio practitioners measuring and tuning sound systems in RTA and transfer-function workspaces.

## Product Purpose

Keep live measurement, alignment, and saved traces accessible in a native Qt measurement tool.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, practical. Preserve the existing instrument-like density and dark/light themes.

## Anti-references

Decorative dashboards, oversized metrics, animated live numbers, and controls that obscure plots.

## Design Principles

- Preserve measurement behavior and existing workflows.
- Keep continuous status visible without workspace changes.
- Distinguish live, stopped, and unavailable readings.
- Reuse existing measurement models and shell tokens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable text in both themes, keyboard-accessible explanations, stable numeric widths, and textual warning states in addition to color. Avoid decorative motion.
